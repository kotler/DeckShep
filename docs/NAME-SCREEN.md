## Related names to assess

[Shepherd](https://www.shepherd.run/) already operates a Claude Code agent-control
product. [Another Shepherd project](https://github.com/ryonakae/shepherd) provides
observability for Herdr-managed agents. These are different full names, but they
are relevant to the same market. Their rights, priority, geographic coverage,
and likelihood of confusion have not been determined. DeckShep must have its own
visual identity and must not imply affiliation with them or Herdr.

Earlier candidates were not adopted. `keyductor.com` has a registry record;
[KeyConductor](https://www.nauta.com/en/news/nauta-group-acquires-majority-stake-in-capturetech-products-bv-/)
is an electronic key-management product, while [Conductor](https://www.conductor.build/)
and [ductor](https://github.com/PleasePrompto/ductor) are related agent software.
The limited Keyductr exact-name checks did not surface an exact software match,
but changing spelling alone does not resolve similar-name questions.

WIPO search is claimed. EUIPO, UKIPO, other national/state registers, business
names, social handles, and common-law use have not been comprehensively searched.
No conclusion about another party's registered rights is made from a product
website or domain record.

Before launch, obtain a clearance review for the actual goods, sales territories,
and final logo. The [USPTO explains](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)
that similar sound, appearance, meaning, commercial impression, and related
goods can matter, and that unregistered common-law rights can still apply.
The release record therefore keeps name clearance pending.
