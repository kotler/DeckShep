## Upstream conclusion

Existing extension points cover the observation/file-exchange experiment. The
reviewed/current public App interfaces do not expose the required connected-Core
lifetime and continuous delivery revisions. Current Core keys and the inspected
Codex settings operation lack the combined captured-context/authorization/deadline
contract. A forwarding mod cannot enforce conditions at a later unconditional
write. The [prototype report](../plans/herdr-app-agent-controls/herdr-model-prototype.md)
identifies the required upstream App and committing-recipient changes, keeps both
routes open and preserves background operation and Rex semantics as requirements.
