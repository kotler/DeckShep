# DeckShep customer license — draft for review

Proposed licensor: Everett Kotler. These terms are a preparation draft and do not
grant a license or offer the product for sale. They must be finalized with the
actual marketplace transaction, seller details, support contact, refund policy,
territories, and applicable consumer requirements before launch.

For a lawfully purchased copy, the proposed license would allow the purchaser to
install and use DeckShep for their own personal or internal business work on
compatible devices they control. It would not transfer ownership of DeckShep or
its original assets. Seat/device limits, transfer rights, updates, and support
duration must be specified in the final offer rather than inferred from this
draft.

Redistribution, resale, sublicensing, or publishing the proprietary code or
original artwork would require written permission, subject to rights that cannot
be restricted by applicable law. Third-party software remains available under its
own licenses. No rights to OpenAI, Anthropic, Elgato, Rex, Herdr, or other parties'
trademarks are granted.

Customers would supply and maintain their own terminal applications, provider
accounts and subscriptions. A DeckShep purchase would not include model usage or
guarantee continued compatibility with third-party products. Supported versions
and preview features must be stated in the listing.

Any final warranty, liability, termination, dispute, refund, and cancellation
language must be reviewed for the actual sales territories and platform terms.
Mandatory consumer rights must remain intact. No jurisdiction, legal entity,
postal address, or working support mailbox is invented in this draft.
